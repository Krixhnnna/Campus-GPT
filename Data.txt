Lovely Professional University (LPU) is a private university located in Chaheru, Phagwara, Punjab, India. 
It was established in 2005 by the Lovely International Trust, under the Lovely Professional University Act, 2005 (Punjab Act No. 25 of 2005) and started operation in 2006.
LPU is one of the largest single-campus universities in India, with more than 30,000 students and over 4,000 faculty and staff members.
The university offers various undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in fields like engineering, management, law, agriculture, design, and more.
LPU has a vast campus spread over 600 acres on the Jalandhar-Delhi G.T. Road.
The mascot of the university is the "Lovely" brand.
LPU is known for its placements, infrastructure, and cultural events.

Placements:
- PEP (Professional Enhancement Program) classes prepare students for placements by teaching specific industry skills like DSA, Web Dev, and Data Science. Industry mentors often teach these regular sessions.
- A special merit-based PEP class, exclusively for 2nd-year CSE students, also features industry mentors and helps students become placement-ready early, allowing them to sit for campus placements in their 5th semester.
- LPU has a strong placement record with companies like Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and Apple recruiting students.
- Highest package offered: Over 64 LPA (Lakhs Per Annum).
- More than 1000+ recruiters visit the campus annually.

Campus Facilities:
